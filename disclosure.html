1. Read before recording or feeding

Pump and Feed Log - MilkLedger records what you enter. It cannot determine whether milk is safe, measure storage temperature or quality, or assess a baby’s health or nutritional needs.

The App does not provide diagnosis, treatment, a recommended feeding amount or a substitute for advice from your pediatrician or feeding/lactation care team. Follow the instructions appropriate to your baby, especially for a premature or unwell baby. Never delay care because of an App display; use local emergency services in an emergency.

2. What Best by means

Best by is an App storage target derived from recorded history, not a measured expiry date or a safety certificate. It assumes correct times, units, locations and continuous appropriate handling. Backdated entries, edits, missing history, mixtures and partial use can affect the result.

Recorded actionApp target
Pump into a new Bottle2 hours from pumping.
Pump into a new Fridge container4 days from pumping, assuming continuous storage at 4°C / 40°F or colder.
Pump or eligible fresh transfer into Freezer6 calendar months from first freezing, assuming −18°C / 0°F or colder.
Freezer → Fridge24 hours from transfer, subject to an earlier applicable target.
Fridge / Freezer → Bottle2 hours from transfer, subject to an earlier applicable target.

These are simplified product rules. CDC describes up to four hours for fresh milk at room temperature under stated conditions, while this App uses a single two-hour Bottle target. CDC’s twenty-four-hour refrigerated thawed-milk window starts at complete thawing; this App starts its timer at the recorded transfer. The App does not sense when thawing completes. CDC’s preferred six-month frozen-storage guidance is not a statement that all milk suddenly becomes unsafe at six months. See CDC storage and preparation guidance and CDC storage questions and answers for conditions and exceptions.

3. Handling decisions remain yours

The App cannot detect contamination, whether a child drank from a bottle, prior warming, interrupted cooling or a device’s actual temperature. A fresh/unused confirmation only records your answer; it does not verify physical conditions.

The App restricts expired-milk movements, refreezing and returns from Bottle to cold storage based on the records available. Recorded milk taken out of cold storage, warmed milk and feeding leftovers are blocked from returning to cold storage. A fresh unused Bottle can be moved only when its recorded history and your answer allow it. These are conservative App restrictions, not a substitute for handling guidance.

Keep different pumping dates in separate containers. Do not add warm fresh milk to chilled or frozen milk. If milk is combined, the earliest applicable App target is retained, but the App cannot verify that mixing was appropriate or identify each source’s exact remaining volume. Never refreeze completely thawed milk.

4. “Don’t use,” Unknown and On hand

Don’t use means the known App Best by target has passed or a known recorded handling restriction has made that milk unavailable for use. Only discard is offered for expired milk. A warning does not physically dispose of milk.

Unknown means there is not enough reliable recorded history for an automatic date. It must not be treated as permission to feed or as a longer storage window. Equally, the absence of Don’t use does not establish safety.

On hand is a record-based amount that excludes milk past a known App target. It is not a physical measurement or a recommended feeding amount. A container can remain in the recorded stock/history until you log a discard; older day-end balances can include amounts that later expired. Trends and stock coverage describe logged activity, not a clinical forecast of supply or a baby’s needs.

5. Watch, widget, reminder and backup limits

A Watch can queue entries offline, and the iPhone may reject or delay them. Check the selected child, pending entries, last-update time and any issue requiring attention. Widgets and summaries may be stale until the operating system refreshes them.

Reminders are optional and can be delayed, silenced or absent because of permissions, Focus, connectivity, power, subscription access or system scheduling. Do not use them as your only storage timer.

Records are local, not held in a developer-operated cloud backup. Keep suitable copies of information you need. Exports can contain sensitive information and are not password-protected by the App. Review restored and corrected records, because their dates and totals may change.

6. Reading acknowledgement and subscriptions

The App stores the version and time of your “I have read and understood” acknowledgement locally. The same information is available in Settings. It is an acknowledgement of reading, not proof that milk is safe, consent to disclose health records, a waiver of mandatory rights, or authorization to purchase.
