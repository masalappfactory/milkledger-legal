4. Important health, storage and feeding disclosure

The App is a recordkeeping tool. It does not determine whether milk is safe to feed, measure its temperature or quality, diagnose or treat a condition, or assess a baby’s nutritional needs.

Best by dates, “Don’t use,” On hand totals, reminders and trends depend on the amounts, dates, containers and handling information entered. Missing, backdated, corrected, duplicated or inaccurate records can change the results. The absence of a warning is not confirmation of safety.

The App uses simplified storage targets: Bottle two hours; fresh Fridge milk four days from pumping; Freezer six months from first freezing; Freezer to Fridge twenty-four hours from transfer; and Fridge or Freezer to Bottle two hours, subject to an earlier applicable limit. These are product rules, not measurements or universal medical expiry dates. CDC guidance counts refrigerated thawed milk’s twenty-four hours from complete thawing, whereas this App starts its simplified timer at transfer. Read the full disclosure and CDC guidance for the distinction and conditions.

The App cannot verify whether milk was promptly chilled, kept continuously cold, previously warmed, contaminated or left after a feed. Answering a handling question does not establish safety. The App may restrict recorded movements, but it cannot prevent physical handling or feeding. Follow current handling guidance and your care team’s instructions, especially for premature or unwell babies. Do not delay medical advice because of an App display. Use local emergency services in an emergency.

On hand excludes milk past a known App Best by date, but this does not physically discard milk or automatically record waste. Historical balances may still include amounts awaiting a discard record. Unknown dates and estimated stock coverage must not be interpreted as feeding recommendations.

The current free allowance is 10 total saved movements, shared across child profiles, the shared stash, iPhone and paired Watch. Each pump, transfer, feed or discard uses one entry. Legacy opening-stock movements also count. Corrections do not consume an additional entry; removed movements remain counted while their audit records are retained. Pending Watch entries can reserve remaining allowance. This is not a monthly allowance.

After the allowance is used, new movements require Premium. Existing history remains readable and correctable, including if a previously saved ledger has more than ten movements. The App derives the counter from its local records. Backups, restores and offline synchronization can affect it. Deleting all App records removes that local history and counter; it does not cancel an Apple subscription. We do not promise recurring allowances or additional trials from reinstalling, deleting or restoring data.
